# Elyrac AI Marketing

AI-powered social media marketing intelligence and content operations platform for Elyrac AI.

## Product principle

AI runs the machinery. Elyrac remains the personality people experience.

## Core workflow

Global Trends & News Intelligence → AI Content Strategist → AI Copywriter → Creative Director + Video Assistant → Authenticity & Brand Check → CEO Approval → Publishing → Analytics → Learning.

## V1

- Dashboard and Today's Campaign
- Approval Inbox
- Content Calendar
- Global Trend & Technology News Intelligence
- Content Studio
- Elyrac Brand Brain
- Authenticity and brand-quality checks
- Platform-specific content variants
- Analytics foundation
- CEO feedback/learning loop

## Guardrails

- Nothing publishes before approval.
- Never fabricate Elyrac experiences, customer outcomes, statistics, partnerships, announcements, pricing, or claims.
- Prefer real company activity, product demonstrations, founder perspective, credible news, and original analysis over generic AI content.
- Content should serve broad global audiences while commercial messaging remains strategically focused.
- Visuals follow the Elyrac brand system and use the Elyrac logo only once, in a top corner.
