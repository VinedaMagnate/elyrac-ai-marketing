# Analytics and marketing intelligence

Elyrac should not optimize content for vanity metrics alone.

The V1 outcome ladder is: Attention → Trust → Conversation → Lead → Opportunity → Client.

Raw platform events remain available, while the intelligence layer gives stronger weight to actions closer to commercial outcomes. This score is a prioritization aid, not a claim of causal attribution or revenue.

Future platform connectors should write normalized events into analytics_events. Strategy recommendations must preserve the distinction between observed performance and inferred explanations.
