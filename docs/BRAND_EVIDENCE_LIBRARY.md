# Brand evidence library

The evidence library gives the strategist factual material about Elyrac rather than encouraging generic invention.

Evidence types include projects, case studies, founder points of view, website material, products, client questions, company updates and approved copy. New evidence may be stored as unapproved. Only evidence explicitly approved for AI use is returned by the Brand Brain to generation workflows.

Evidence approval means the material may be used as source context. It does not mean every detail is public, publishable or suitable for every campaign. Sensitive/client material still requires campaign-level judgment and CEO approval.
