# Elyrac AI Marketing — Agent Architecture

## Principle
AI runs the machinery; Elyrac remains the personality people experience.

## Pipeline
1. Trend Intelligence gathers verifiable global technology/business signals.
2. Strategist combines those signals with real Elyrac activity and marketing objectives.
3. Copywriter creates native platform variants.
4. Creative Director and Video Assistant produce execution directions.
5. Authenticity Engine rejects generic, fabricated or weakly evidenced material.
6. CEO Approval is mandatory.
7. Publisher sends only approved content to supported platform integrations.
8. Analytics learns which content creates attention, trust, enquiries and qualified opportunities.

## Evidence contract
A campaign must retain its source evidence. Current events must have source URLs and timestamps. Internal Elyrac claims must come from approved Brand Brain/project evidence. The agents must not convert inference into fact.

## Approval contract
No publish action may be called from generation. Generation and publishing are deliberately separated. Sensitive claims, customer information, offers, pricing, partnerships and announcements require explicit CEO approval.

## Authenticity rule
If Elyrac AI can be replaced by another AI company's name without materially changing the content, the draft should be treated as weak and revised.
